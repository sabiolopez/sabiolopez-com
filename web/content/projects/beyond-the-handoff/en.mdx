  Engineering at Ticket Edenred formed a team with two front-end developers and selected me as the designer responsible for the POC. Together, we studied and validated the design workflow. I led the design work, preparing components and instructions to test how GitHub Copilot could use Figma context through MCP to create and edit interfaces.

  My design work focused on the structure agents received: components, tokens, layer naming, Auto Layout, and responsive behavior. The proof of concept also explored how Design and Engineering could evolve the Delaunay Design System together.

  With the POC validated and approved, I’m now a point of contact for designers in the tribe who want to prototype with AI and MCP.

  <ProcessGrid>
    <ProcessStep
      number="01"
      title="Prototyping with Figma MCP and GitHub Copilot"

    />
    <ProcessStep
      number="02"
      title="Design system reconstruction"
      description="To establish a reliable foundation, we used AI-assisted workflows to rebuild the Delaunay DS Figma library, drawing on existing code components and Storybook documentation. The aim was to reduce discrepancies between design and code before experimenting with automation."
    />
    <ProcessStep
      number="03"
      title="Connecting design and code"
      description="We used Code Connect to map Figma components to their code counterparts. This was intended to support the reuse of existing components rather than generating new implementations based solely on appearance."
    />
    <ProcessStep
      number="04"
      title="Designing for agents"
      description="On the Design side, I worked on structuring deliverables for AI-assisted consumption: semantic layer naming, existing component usage, design tokens, Auto Layout, and responsive behavior. Structural quality became as important to the workflow as visual quality."
    />
    <ProcessStep
      number="05"
      title="Agent skills and orchestration"
      description="The initiative organized recurring activities into specialized skills covering design authoring, auditing, handoff, and orchestration. The Engineering inventory also covered project context, Figma-based implementation, business rules, service integration, and orchestration. The goal was to make instructions contextual and reusable instead of relying on isolated prompts."
    />
    <ProcessStep
      number="06"
      title="Shared system evolution"
      description="The scope expanded to design system maintenance. We explored Figma branching to create and update components, with designers and front-end engineers involved in reviewing proposed changes."
    />
    <ProcessStep
      number="07"
      title="Four prototyping scenarios"
      description="We exercised component creation and modification, full-page creation, and editing a section of an existing page."
    />
    <ProcessStep
      number="08"
      title="Training designers after validation"
      description="With the POC approved, I took responsibility for presenting the workflow, training designers on other teams within the tribe, and helping them apply it to their own projects. This phase also includes sharing the approach with designers in other tribes."
